# Personal site / Substack comparison

Checked September 13, 2026 against the live [Posts archive](https://vanshkumar.net/posts/). All 15 site posts were matched to 16 Substack articles: Apartment planning combines two articles, and Preordained in the present comes from a section of Crusade.

The main findings are two missing videos, a missing closing section in Inscrutable exhortations, two posts with visible Markdown artifacts, and one broken site-only link. Preordained in the present is an intact excerpt from a longer original; whether the surrounding material should be imported is an editorial choice.

No post content was changed during this audit.

## Smaller differences

- **Terminal:** Substack uses a line break between the two sentences beginning “If life is long” and “And if life is short.” The site uses separate paragraphs in the blockquote, which gives them more vertical space. No text is lost.
- **Aliveline:** Old `/traces/` and `/logs/` links were converted to the site's current `/notes/` and nested post-log routes. `quest(ion)s` gains a `/now` link. Body headings were changed from H1/H4 to H2/H3; cross-post and trailing subscribe separators were dropped.
- **Virtue:** Its two Substack section links were converted to working local anchors. Split adjacent bold spans were consolidated without losing the emphasized prose. Heading levels were adjusted to the site's hierarchy; the References heading no longer has a redundant nested bold tag.
- **Arabian Nights, footnote 3:** The original splits each formula label across inconsistent WolframAlpha links, including a duplicated URL. The site makes each complete label one link, using the corresponding original `) case` destination. Footnote prose is unchanged. This is a deliberate link correction recorded in the import notes, not a lost reference.
- **Subtitles:** Number-only subtitles are omitted for Apartment planning, Arabian Nights, Train Person, Divorce kitchen, and the Crusade excerpt. Aliveline and Inscrutable exhortations gain site subtitles. Partition Summer's subtitle starts with lowercase `a` on the site. Other subtitles match.
- **Dates:** The historical essay dates match Substack in Pacific time. Apartment planning uses January 23, 2025, the second part's publication day; the first part was January 22. Site-originated TJ, Partition Summer, and Aliveline have earlier site start dates than their Substack publication dates. Impact displays a September 13, 2026 modification date, distinct from its December 8, 2024 publication date.

## Image fidelity

All 40 corresponding images checked were reachable, including the comic-reader cover. Twenty-four of the 39 ordinary article image pairs are pixel-identical to the original uploaded files. The remaining pairs, plus the cover, show encoding/color or resizing differences consistent with importing Substack's served image versions; no substituted image was found. The pair with the largest thumbnail difference was also visually inspected and depicts the same complete comic.

## Scope and method

Compared public live HTML, not just the local Markdown or search-engine extracts. Checked body text, paragraph/heading structure, emphasis, image order/content, captions, text-link destinations, footnotes, in-page anchors, subtitles, and dates. All **16 footnotes** have matching normalized text, and all local article fragment links resolve to existing IDs. Subscription forms, publication navigation, comments, reactions, share controls, and recommendation widgets were excluded. Authored feedback buttons were retained in the comparison.

Browser checks confirmed Impact's rendered artifacts, the train video, the comic reader, and the workflow-link 404. Image comparisons used fetched assets, dimensions, pixel equality, reduced-size image differences, and a visual check of the largest mismatch. This was not a full playback test of videos, a pixel-by-pixel layout comparison at every viewport, or a general availability audit of every external reference destination.
