The Baby book’s view of screens

The Montessori Baby recommends avoiding screens before age two and having little or none after two. The authors favor direct, sensory exploration over passive screen entertainment.

In the everyday

When you are considering the place of screens in family life.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The authors recommend no screens before two and little or none after two.

This describes the Baby book’s perspective on family screen use.

The authors describe babies learning through their hands and mouths, with simple things to explore and time for their own discoveries. They also make room for quiet in everyday life.

Their screen recommendation appears in a discussion of concentration. It is the Baby authors’ position, alongside their preference for active experience; it is not a promise about how an individual child will develop.
