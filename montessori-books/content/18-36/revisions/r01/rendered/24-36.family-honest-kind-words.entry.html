A perspective

Honest words can still be kind

Both books invite adults to model truthfulness with sensitivity. Davies offers everyday examples of stating a limit or making a truthful observation without an invented excuse.

In the everyday

When you are tempted to give a convenient excuse rather than say what you mean.

For a call she does not want to take, Davies suggests saying that she is tired and asking to speak later. Her example makes the adult’s limit clear without pretending to be occupied with something else.

When asked about a haircut she does not like, she offers a truthful observation about the person’s pleasure in it. The point is to look for words that are both accurate and considerate.

The Baby authors likewise include sensitive truthfulness in their adaptation of don Miguel Ruiz’s Four Agreements. Davies acknowledges that finding kind, honest words takes practice.
