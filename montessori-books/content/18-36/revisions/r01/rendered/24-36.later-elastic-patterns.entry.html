A perspective

Stretch a band between pegs

Davies describes a pegboard and elastic bands for an older toddler to stretch and explore with adult supervision. A pattern can emerge, but copying a design is optional.

In the everyday

Your child is interested in stretching a band and hooking it over a peg.

Age, interest & context

In the book: From around 2 years in the illustrated example; 2+ years in the appendix.

Interest in stretching bands over pegs; making patterns belongs to a later use, not a requirement.

The illustrated example begins with stretching elastic bands over pegs and is labeled from around 2 years. Older children may use the same material to make patterns.

Show the movement slowly if help is wanted, then leave the arrangement to the child. This is one possible hand activity, not equipment every family needs. Small parts require supervision.
