An everyday situation

Find common ground with a caregiver

For everyday differences, choose what matters most and make a clear, kind request. Leave room to hear the other person’s concern and to find a way forward together.

In the everyday

When you and another trusted caregiver want to talk about different approaches.

Age, interest & context

General guidance for family life with a toddler; no skill is expected at this age.

It can help to agree on a time when you are both able to talk. Describe the situation and what matters to you, then listen to the other person’s perspective. Both books favor requests that leave room for another solution.

You can share a small piece of information or an experience if the other person is interested. Agreement on the big priorities can coexist with different ways of playing, comforting, or enjoying time together.

The books also value what caregivers bring: their interests, stories, culture, and companionship. Notice and appreciate the concrete ways they help.

What’s underneath

Shared priorities can leave room for different caring relationships.
