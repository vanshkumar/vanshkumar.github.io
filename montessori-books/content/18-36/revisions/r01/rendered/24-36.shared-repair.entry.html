An everyday situation

Try again after a hard moment

When you have spoken or acted in a way you regret, you can acknowledge it, apologize, and say what you would like to do differently. Both books make room for mistakes and another attempt.

In the everyday

After you have lost patience or let someone down.

Age, interest & context

General guidance for family life with a toddler; no skill is expected at this age.

Keep the focus on your own words or actions. Davies describes taking responsibility for losing her temper rather than blaming the child for it.

The Baby authors include apology and naming what could have been said or done differently, even while caring for a baby. They also invite forgiveness and learning from mistakes.

Repair can be part of how a family lives together. It does not depend on being a parent who gets every moment right.

What’s underneath

Taking responsibility and making amends can be part of everyday family life.
