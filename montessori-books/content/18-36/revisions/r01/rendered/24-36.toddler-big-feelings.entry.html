An everyday situation

Stay available through a hard feeling

Davies describes staying available during a tantrum, offering comfort without insisting on touch, and keeping everyone safe. Discussion comes after calm, with help to make amends if someone was hurt or something was damaged.

Age, interest & context

Your child is overwhelmed by a disappointment, frustration, or a limit.

An offered cuddle may help, or your child may push it away. Davies keeps the adult nearby and available when touch is unwelcome, with immediate action to stop harm.

She suggests noticing situations that can be difficult—frustration, events not going as hoped, wanting control, or trouble communicating—and anticipating them when possible. These are possibilities to understand, not a diagnosis or a promise to prevent every tantrum.

In public, she describes moving out of the way when possible. If the family must leave, she retains gentle adult help and acknowledgment of the child’s experience.
