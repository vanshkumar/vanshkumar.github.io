The conversation inside everyday care

Davies treats dressing, washing, diapering, and meals as moments to be with a child. She keeps respectful touch and communication within necessary care.

She describes talking about what is happening, listening even before words, and allowing the child a turn in the exchange. An ordinary care moment can include a smile and shared attention.

Her respect for a child’s say in touch sits alongside the adult’s responsibility for necessary care and protection. She describes gentle help and brief explanation when care must happen.
