A perspective

A part in washing hands

Davies includes handwashing among the care routines toddlers can join, with help as their skills grow. The child does not have to manage every part.

In the everyday

Your child may want a turn with the soap, the towel, or a movement they have watched you make.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The chart includes these care activities through 3 years, with help as skills grow.

Your child may want a turn with the soap, the towel, or a movement they have watched you make.

Her sink example keeps soap and a towel with the routine. She pairs handwashing with other gradually shared care, such as hair brushing and nose wiping.

The book also describes a separate handwashing table for a child who wants to repeat washing at the sink and can carry a jug. That is a different setup, not something every family needs to create. Ordinary washing together can offer a part to join.
