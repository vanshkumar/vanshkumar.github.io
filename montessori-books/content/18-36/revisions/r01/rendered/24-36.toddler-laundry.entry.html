A small invitation

Carry a shirt with the laundry

Invite your child to carry one piece of clothing while you carry the rest. A familiar household task becomes something you share.

Start by noticing

Notice an interest in carrying clothes, filling the laundry basket, or looking for matching socks.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The chapter gives an 18-month-old carrying a T-shirt; sorting appears at 18 months to 3 years; hanging cloths is 18+ months in the appendix.

Interest in carrying clothing, sorting socks or colors, or using pegs with supervision; participation may stay small.

Carrying and sorting remain useful here. The separate 3+ folding row does not make folding part of this entry.

Davies gives the example of an 18-month-old carrying a T-shirt while the adult carries the trousers. Sorting socks or clothing by color can be another small part, when it interests your child.

Her laundry examples also include hanging small wet cloths with pegs. That adds a different hand movement and needs supervision for small parts; it need not be added to a simple carrying turn.

Try it together

  1. Offer one item to carry to the laundry basket.
  2. Carry your part alongside them.
  3. Leave another small part available if they want to continue.
