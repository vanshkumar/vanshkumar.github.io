A small invitation

Join in with a tissue or brush

A tissue or hairbrush in a familiar place can let your child join a small part of caring for themselves.

Start by noticing

Notice whether your child reaches for the tissue or brush, or watches you use one.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The chart includes these care activities through 3 years, with help as skills grow.

Notice whether your child reaches for the tissue or brush, or watches you use one.

Davies suggests showing how you wipe your own nose, then offering the child a turn. A few tissues, a reachable mirror, and a place for used tissues support the routine.

For hair, she pairs a brush with a mirror. Keep help available and show the movement slowly; your child does not need to manage the whole routine alone.
