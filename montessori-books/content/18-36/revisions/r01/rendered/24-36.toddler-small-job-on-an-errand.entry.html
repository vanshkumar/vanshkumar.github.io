A small invitation

Helping with the shopping

Invite your child to join one useful part of an errand, such as looking at a picture shopping list or helping put an item on the checkout belt.

Start by noticing

They may want to be involved in what you are doing, especially during a wait or transition.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Shopping participation is a practical-life example; the home chart includes it at 18 months to 3 years.

Interest in a real part of shopping, such as a picture list or passing an item; adult help and time remain available.

Davies includes shopping among ordinary opportunities to contribute. A picture list can let a child look for what comes next with you, and unpacking can continue the shared job at home.

Keep the part manageable and leave time for help. The task need not keep your child occupied for the whole outing; toddlers also need movement and connection while they wait.
