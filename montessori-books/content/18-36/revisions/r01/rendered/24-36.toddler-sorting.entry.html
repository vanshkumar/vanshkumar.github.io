A perspective

Noticing what belongs together

Davies describes toddlers grouping things by type, color, or size from around 18 months. The interest in likeness is the starting point, with an adult supervising small materials.

In the everyday

Notice your child bringing similar things together or sorting clothes during an ordinary task.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Around 18 months in the chapter; 18+ months in the appendix; sorting small buttons is illustrated from 2 years.

Interest in likenesses, types, colors, or sizes; supervised small objects and ordinary clothing are distinct examples.

The chapter proposes a group of objects and bowls or a divided container. The appendix gives two kinds of an item as a simple comparison. Your child can explore the differences without being quizzed for the right answer.

The small-button photograph is labeled from 2 years, later than the broader sorting discussion. It does not set the materials for every 18-month-old. Sorting socks or clothing is also part of the book’s household participation examples.
