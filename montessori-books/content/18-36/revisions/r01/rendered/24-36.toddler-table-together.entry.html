A home within reach

← Three starting ideas

A small invitation

Help the table get ready

Invite your child to bring a manageable item to the table with you. Setting or clearing can be a small shared part of the meal.

Start by noticing

Notice interest in carrying things to the table or putting them back after a meal.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Setting and clearing the table at 18 months to 3 years in the home chart; 18+ months in the appendix.

Interest in carrying something to or from the table; help and a manageable item or basket.

Davies keeps a small set of table things in a reachable place and uses a basket to carry them. A picture showing where the plate, glass, and cutlery belong is an optional guide.

Show slow carrying with two hands for a glass or tray, and help as needed. The child might carry one thing while you do the rest. This is a chance to contribute, not a requirement to set a whole table.
