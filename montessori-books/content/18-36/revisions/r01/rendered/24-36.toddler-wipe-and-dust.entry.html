A small invitation

Wiping and dusting together

Offer a cloth for wiping or dusting alongside you, with help when your child needs it.

Start by noticing

Notice your child copying a wiping or sweeping movement or asking to join you.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Dusting and sweeping at 16–18+ months in the appendix; the home chart continues them at 18 months to 3 years.

Interest in wiping or copying household work; keep help and cleaning tools manageable.

Keep the cloth or hand mitt where the task happens, with help ready. For an interested child, Davies also describes a manageable broom and dustpan, with a marked place to gather the dirt.

The point is joining the work. It will often be slower and less thorough, and you can stop before either of you feels overwhelmed. Returning the cloth can be a shared last step.
