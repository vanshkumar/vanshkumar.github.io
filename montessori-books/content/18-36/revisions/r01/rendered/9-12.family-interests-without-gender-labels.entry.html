A perspective

Let interests grow beyond gender labels

The Baby authors invite adults to offer language and opportunities that do not limit a child’s interests by gender. They suggest seeing each child as capable.

In the everyday

When choosing words, clothes, books, or things your child might explore.

Age, interest & context

An adult perspective on the words and opportunities offered during the early years.

Notice whether a quality or activity is being offered only to one gender. The book gives examples of caring boys, strong girls, boys interested in dolls, and girls interested in vehicles. These are possibilities, not interests a child has to show.

The authors also suggest describing a temporary difficulty instead of making it the child’s identity. What is happening in this moment need not become a label for who they are.
