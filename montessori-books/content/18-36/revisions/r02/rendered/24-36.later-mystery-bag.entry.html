A perspective

Find a familiar shape by touch

Davies describes a mystery bag for a child interested in recognizing an object by touch. She places this interest around 2½ in the chapter; small objects need adult supervision.

In the everyday

Your child wants to feel around an object without looking at it.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Around 2.5 years in the chapter and illustrated example; around 2+ years in the appendix.

Interest in identifying an object by touch; distinct shapes are easier to distinguish.

This broad band includes the earlier and later parts of the third year; follow the stated interest and help, not the birthday alone.

Choose objects with clearly different shapes, such as a spoon and a key, in a bag that is hard to peek into. The child can feel for an object you name or discover what is inside by touch.

Her examples use objects on a theme or matching pairs; a mixture of unrelated objects is harder. The appendix suggests five to eight objects and labels the activity around 2 years and older. These are differing starting descriptions, not a test to complete at either age.
