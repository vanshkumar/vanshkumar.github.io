A perspective

When a pedal tricycle becomes interesting

Davies lists a pedal tricycle as a later riding possibility, from 2½ years. It is an option to consider through your child’s interest and current movement, not a skill to reach on that birthday.

In the everyday

Your child is interested in riding and a different way of moving may be appealing.

Age, interest & context

In the book: From 2.5 years for the pedal-tricycle variant; the earlier feet-propelled examples share a14–16-month appendix row.

Follow interest and current movement; the source does not give a separate pedal-readiness test.

This source example belongs to the later part of the third year, not its beginning.

The same appendix row first describes a low trike or balance bike moved along with the feet. Pedaling is a different, later example; the age label alone does not replace watching what the child can manage.

Her wider movement discussion makes space for many ways to move. Riding need not replace running, dancing, or playing with a ball, and the examples are not a list of equipment every home needs.
