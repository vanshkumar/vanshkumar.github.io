A perspective

How Davies introduces scissors

Davies describes supervised scissors practice while seated at a table: learning to hold the handles, then snipping a narrow strip. Her chapter begins around 18 months; her illustrated example and appendix say around 2 years.

In the everyday

Your child is interested in opening and closing the scissors with your supervision.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Around 18 months in chapter prose; around 2 years in the illustrated example; around 2+ years in the appendix.

Seated supervised handling, beginning with opening and closing; the adult holds the card at first.

She specifies real scissors with rounded ends that cut well. At first a child may use two hands to open and close them. When introducing cutting, the adult holds a thin strip of sturdy card for the child to snip; holding both scissors and paper comes later as hand strength develops.

The cut pieces can go into a bowl and then a little envelope closed with a sticker. Repeating the opening-and-closing movement can be the whole exploration for now. Supervision remains part of this activity at either age.
