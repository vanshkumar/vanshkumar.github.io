A perspective

Fitting shapes and opening lids

Davies’s hand activities follow familiar movements: fitting, releasing, turning, and using two hands together. The useful choice is the movement your child wants to repeat.

In the everyday

They may be returning to the same lid, shape, peg, or ring.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Related appendix examples have different starting ages: rings and opening/closing at 12+ months; posting and box variants at 14+ months.

Interest in fitting or turning; simplify what is offered and retain small-part supervision.

Her examples include rings on a peg, shapes through an opening, and boxes with sliding lids or bins. A child can explore taking pieces off before putting them back. Color or size matching may come later within the same material.

She suggests simplifying a shape sorter to an easier shape, then adding challenge when it is welcome. These are material examples, not a required collection. Small parts require adult supervision; the book does not make every household closure or loose object suitable for a toddler.
