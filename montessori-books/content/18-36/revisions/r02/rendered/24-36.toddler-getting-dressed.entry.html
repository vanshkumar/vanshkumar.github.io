An everyday situation

“I want to do it” when it is time to dress

Let your child try a small part of dressing, with help when it is wanted or needed. The amount they want to do can change from day to day.

In the everyday

They may reach for their clothes, resist help, or ask you to do something they managed yesterday.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The chart includes these care activities through 3 years, with help as skills grow.

They may reach for their clothes, resist help, or ask you to do something they managed yesterday.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 33, 51–52.

Davies suggests easy openings and clothes that allow movement, with a limited choice of seasonally suitable things within reach. Elastic waists and a roomy neck opening are examples.

Break dressing into small parts. You might put a shirt over their head and leave the sleeves for them to try, or hold the back of a shoe while they put a foot in. Offer a little help when they are becoming frustrated.

When you must leave, acknowledge the wish to do it alone and explain that you will help finish. Davies keeps gentle handling and connection alongside the adult’s time boundary.
