Room for your toddler beside a new baby

Davies makes room for a toddler’s difficult feelings and ordinary time together when a new baby joins the family. The adult still steps in immediately to prevent harm.

In the everyday

Your toddler may want closeness, want to help, or want little to do with the baby.

Age, interest & context

Your toddler may want closeness, want to help, or want little to do with the baby.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

The book suggests keeping the early weeks simple and accepting extra hands when available. Books or familiar toys close by can give you something to share with your toddler while feeding the baby.

Fetching a clean diaper may interest a toddler; declining to help is allowed too. A brief walk or ordinary errand together can offer time with you.

If the toddler expresses anger or jealousy, Davies listens rather than denying the feeling. She is equally clear that hitting the baby must be stopped immediately.
