A perspective

Room for the movement they choose

Davies places everyday movement beside quiet hand work: dancing, running, and playing with a ball are among her examples. The child’s current movement guides the choice.

In the everyday

Notice which movements your child seeks and where the available space helps or gets in the way.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Toddler-wide movement discussion; specific equipment examples carry differing ages and support.

Choose from the movements the child is seeking rather than treating the whole list as skills to do.

The entry gives a broad movement perspective; it does not prescribe the book’s full equipment list at this age.

The activity chapter makes room for movement inside when space permits and outdoors. Its ball example favors outdoor space for rolling and kicking.

The book’s longer equipment lists include climbing, sliding, balancing, and riding, with differing age and readiness descriptions. They are possibilities to understand in context, rather than a set of skills to complete at this age.
