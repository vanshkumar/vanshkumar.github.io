A home within reach

← Three starting ideas

A small invitation

A place for shoes

A reachable basket gives shoes a familiar home. Invite your child to fetch them or put them away with you.

Start by noticing

Notice whether your child looks for their shoes or wants to help when you leave or come home.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Fetching shoes appears at 12 to 18 months; the chart adds other activities at 18 months to 3 years.

Interest in fetching or returning shoes; reachable storage and a seat support participation.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 48–50, 52.

Choose a consistent place your child can reach. A seat nearby can make putting shoes on and taking them off easier to join.

Show the small movements slowly. Fetching one shoe or returning it to the basket may be their part today; you can carry the other. On another day, more help may be welcome.

Try it together

  1. Keep shoes in one reachable basket or on a shelf.
  2. Invite your child to bring a shoe when you get ready.
  3. Show where it belongs when you return, and put the shoes away together.
