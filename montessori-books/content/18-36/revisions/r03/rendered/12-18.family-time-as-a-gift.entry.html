A perspective

Give time with something you enjoy

A song, a shared book, or a story from your own life can be a gift of time. Both books value what caregivers enjoy sharing; Davies also recognizes that giving a present can express love.

In the everyday

When a relative or caregiver wants a way to share something with your child.

The Baby authors describe grandparents and caregivers bringing music, stories, history, and culture into family life. Their letter to visitors invites adults to share their own interests.

Davies suggests choosing an interest that can be shared with the child, or spending time together outdoors. A book enjoyed together is another of her examples.

Her gift suggestions emphasize being together and can also include giving a parent some respite. Paid outings are among her examples, but a purchased experience is not required to share a song or story.
