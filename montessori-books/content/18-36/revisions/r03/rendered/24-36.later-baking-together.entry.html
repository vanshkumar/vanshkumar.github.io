A perspective

A stir while you bake

Davies includes older toddlers in baking through measuring with help, stirring, and cleaning up together. The appendix begins this example at 2½ years and older.

In the everyday

Your child is interested in adding or stirring ingredients while you work together.

Age, interest & context

In the book: 2.5+ years in the appendix; measuring and mixing appear at 3 to 4 years in the home chart.

Interest in a supervised shared part of preparation; help remains available.

This broad band includes the earlier and later parts of the third year; follow the stated interest and help, not the birthday alone.

The practical-life chapter treats baking as shared work: taking turns, helping add ingredients, and stirring. A child can join the movement that interests them while the adult continues the rest.

The book’s home chart places measuring and mixing at 3 to 4 years, later than the appendix’s 2½+ baking example. These are different descriptions of help and task complexity, not a deadline to measure independently.

Keep expectations about the finished result modest and stop before it becomes too much. This entry describes joining the preparation; it does not set out a recipe or a role at the oven.
