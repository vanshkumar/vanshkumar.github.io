A perspective

The book’s calm-place idea near age three

Davies describes a child-chosen calm place near age three. Her prose says around 3, while the recap says over 3; this is not a setup instruction for every child in the 2–3-year band.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Older toddler around 3 years old in PDF136; child over 3 years old in PDF138.

The source specifies voluntary use, not a readiness test; these differing ages do not establish suitability below 3.

Included as book context near the third birthday, with no under-three calm-space procedure.

The idea is attributed to Jane Nelsen. In Davies’s description, a child chooses whether to use the space and how long to stay. It is never a threat or an imposed time-out, and an adult can offer to go along.

The prose and recap also describe suggesting more time there if a child remains upset. That sits within the wider guidance to stay available, offer rather than insist on touch, and act immediately to stop harm. It does not make comfort conditional on being calm.

For the toddler years, Davies’s recurring sequence is support through the feeling, reconnection after calm, and help making amends when needed. The differing calm-place ages should not become a reason to send a younger child away.
