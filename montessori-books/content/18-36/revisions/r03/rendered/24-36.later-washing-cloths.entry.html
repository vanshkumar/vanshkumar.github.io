A perspective

Wash a cloth together

Davies describes washing cloths with an adult as a chance to repeat scrubbing, washing, and cleanup movements. One part of the work can hold a child’s interest.

In the everyday

Your child wants to scrub a cloth or repeat part of washing it.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Around 2+ years in the appendix.

Interest in scrubbing or part of a washing sequence, with supervision and help.

The appendix example has two tubs on a table, a small scrub board, a bar of soap in a dish, a jug, and baskets beside the table. An apron, drying mitt, and hand-drying cloth complete her setup.

Show slowly and help where needed. A child may keep returning to one movement rather than finish the whole sequence. Davies advises limiting what is put out and stopping before the shared work becomes overwhelming.
