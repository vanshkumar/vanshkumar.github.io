A small invitation

Try the coat flip when there is time

If your child wants to put on a coat, a quiet practice moment can make room to learn its movements with your help.

Start by noticing

Notice an interest in getting a coat on; choose a moment when you are not rushing out.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The chart includes these care activities through 3 years, with help as skills grow.

Notice an interest in getting a coat on; choose a moment when you are not rushing out.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 33, 51.

Davies’s coat flip starts with an open coat on the floor, its inside facing up, and the child at the hood or label end. Hands go into the sleeves, then the arms lift overhead so the coat moves onto the body.

You can show it slowly and help with the part that is difficult. Trying a movement is enough; getting ready can still be a shared task.

Try it together

  1. Lay the coat open on the floor, inside facing up, with your child at the hood or label end.
  2. Show how hands go into the sleeves.
  3. Help as needed as the arms lift overhead and the coat comes onto the body.
