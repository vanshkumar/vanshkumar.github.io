Age, interest & context

In the book: Home play is unaged within the toddler chapter; the separate creativity discussion places pretend play around 2.5+ years.

Interest in open-ended familiar toys; later pretend play can draw on real daily experience.

The start of this band does not imply pretend play should have begun; Davies’s later description remains around 2½ and older. Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 33.

At home, Davies makes room for imaginative scenes from daily life. In her separate creativity discussion, she describes pretend play as processing experience and places its beginning around 2½ years and later.

Her home examples include blocks, farm animals, trains, and vehicles. These are possibilities within a varied collection, not a shopping list. Materials still need to fit the child using them, with supervision for small parts.

If a child attends a Montessori classroom, she makes room at home for daily life, outdoors, creativity, and rest without duplicating all the school materials.
