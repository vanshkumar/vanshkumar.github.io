A perspective

A part in preparing food

Davies includes toddlers in food preparation through small, supervised contributions. Rinsing or tearing salad leaves is one of her examples for a young helper.

In the everyday

Your child may want to join what you are making, even if only briefly.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The home chart includes food preparation at 18 months to 3 years, with help; sharp knives remain out of reach and the preschool knife example is later.

Interest in contributing with adult help; food preparation remains supervised.

The body preserves separate tool and age descriptions; this age does not authorize all listed cutting tasks.

She suggests tasks suited to small hands, with adult help at first and room to wander off when interest ends. Food preparation can be brought to a low or dining table.

The book goes on to describe spreading, peeling, cutting, and squeezing, with different materials and age labels. Apple cutting in the detailed photograph is labeled from 2 years and includes hands-on adult assistance; the earlier table also mentions assisted apple preparation.

Davies requires supervision for activities with sharp tools. Her kitchen section keeps sharp knives out of reach and describes introducing sharper knives with a cutting guide as children become preschoolers, with supervision. Preparing food remains something to do together.
