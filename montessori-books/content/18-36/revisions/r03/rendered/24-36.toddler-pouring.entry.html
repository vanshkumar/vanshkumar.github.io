A small invitation

A little water to pour

If your child is interested in pouring and can manage a small jug, offer a little water with a cloth nearby. Stay available to help.

Start by noticing

Notice an interest in tipping, filling, or pouring, and whether the jug feels manageable in their hands.

Age, interest & context

In the book: 12 to 18 months in the home chart; 18+ months in the appendix, with earlier activities continuing into later ages.

Interest in pouring; a manageable small jug, a little liquid, and help for spills.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 48–50, 52.

A small amount of water makes the invitation easier to share. Davies includes pouring in ordinary family life, with a little jug and something ready for spills.

Show the movement slowly, then leave room for a turn. Your child may want to repeat just the pouring or the wiping. You can help with the rest.

Try it together

  1. Set out a small jug with a little water, a glass, and a cloth or sponge for spills.
  2. Show one slow pour, then offer a turn.
  3. Wipe up together when needed.
