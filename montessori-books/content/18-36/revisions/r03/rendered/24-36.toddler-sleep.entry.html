What bedtime asks of your family

Davies frames sleep as a personal family question, with a familiar bedtime sequence and the help the child needs. She advises specialist help when continuing difficulties affect the child or family.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Her bedtime discussion makes space for ordinary care, books, and conversation before sleep. She explicitly rejects leaving a child to cry alone and notes that illness or teething may call for additional support.

The chapter also describes particular settling methods, sleeping spaces, and changes to feeding at night. Those passages depend on more than a toddler’s age and are not a general bedtime recipe.

Her closing advice is to see a sleep specialist when persistent difficulty affects the well-being of the child or family.
