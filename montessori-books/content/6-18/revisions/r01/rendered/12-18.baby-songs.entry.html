A small invitation

Make a moment for a song

Sing a familiar song or listen to a short piece together. Notice a look, a sound, or a movement that joins in.

Start by noticing

Your baby is interested in your voice or the rhythm they hear.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The song variation appears at 6–9 months; appendix music and rhythmic-language rows say All ages.

Interest in the voice or music, with a look, sound or movement in response.

The activity may continue while it fits your child’s interest and current skills; the source ages are guides.

You can try the same song a little slower or with a different voice and see what holds their interest. A short rhyme with a familiar gesture is another possibility.

The authors make space for quiet between musical moments. Music need not run all day, and an instrument is not needed for sharing a song.
