A small invitation

Sing, move, and answer a rhythm

Share a song you enjoy, copy a movement your child makes, or listen to a piece of music together. There is no need to perform well.

Start by noticing

Notice a sway, a clap, a repeated sound, or a wish to hear a song again.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The appendix says“all ages” within this toddler book.

Interest in shared songs, rhythm, sounds or movement.

Davies includes spontaneous songs with actions, family and cultural dances, and copying the rhythm a child makes. You can make room for their response as well as offer your own.

Listening can be an activity in itself. Give the music a moment of attention, instead of always leaving it behind other noise. Short rhymes with finger or body actions are another way to share rhythm.
