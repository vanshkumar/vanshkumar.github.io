A small invitation

A familiar thing and its picture

Name a few familiar objects together. If matching interests your child, offer a picture that looks just like the object.

Start by noticing

Notice interest in naming things, recognizing pictures, or finding a familiar object in a book.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Vocabulary objects:12+ months; matching objects with cards:14+ months. The separate vocabulary-card row says 18+ months.

Start with naming and exploring objects; matching follows interest, and similar pictures follow identical matches.

Picture cards without matching objects are a later vocabulary example. Formal naming tests are for children over 3 in this book.

Davies groups vocabulary objects around a familiar theme. A photograph of the actual object can make the match clear; a similar but different picture is a later challenge once identical matching is familiar.

Say the names and enjoy finding a match. If an object lands on a different picture, notice it without turning the moment into a test, and return to naming another time. The book reserves its formal naming test for children over 3.

Picture cards on a familiar theme can also extend the words you share. Stay with your child when using small real or replica objects; the book’s small-part supervision condition still applies.
