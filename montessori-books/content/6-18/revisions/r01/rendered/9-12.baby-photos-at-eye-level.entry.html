Familiar pictures where your baby can see

Start by noticing

You are looking at what your baby can see from where they spend time.

Age, interest & context

In the book: No fixed age; pictures are placed at the child’s height.

The child can see a familiar picture from where they spend time.

The authors suggest a couple of pictures of familiar people, animals or nature. Personal, handmade touches can belong beside them; the room need not follow one neutral style.

This is about a picture being visible from the baby’s position. The book does not make an elaborate display a requirement.
