A perspective

A basket of things to explore

For a baby who gets into sitting independently, the authors describe a discovery basket of a few varied objects, each suitable for grasping and mouthing with all parts secure.

In the everyday

Sitting leaves your baby’s hands free, and they are interested in choosing and examining objects.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Main basket example: 6–9-month section after independent sitting. Familiar-object appendix basket: 5 or 6+ months.

Independent sitting for the seated discovery basket; simple familiar-object variant also allows lying.

The activity may continue while it fits your child’s interest and current skills; the source ages are guides.

Their examples include a small group of kitchen objects with different shapes or textures. The baby chooses what to examine; an occasional name can fit without interrupting every discovery.

A separate appendix example uses two or three familiar toys in a soft basket while the baby lies or sits. That simpler choice is distinct from propping a baby to use a seated discovery basket.
