A small invitation

Leave room for another try

When your child is safely absorbed, give them room to watch, repeat, or stay with one part of an activity. Stay available when they need you.

Start by noticing

When you are tempted to move things along because the same action is happening again.

A quiet moment can hold plenty of interest. Watch the child’s attention rather than using your own sense of boredom to decide when to change activities.

Both books describe children repeating one part of an activity. They also suggest allowing a moment to take in what you have said or shown before repeating it yourself.

This room for concentration includes noticing when your child is frustrated or needs care. It can sit alongside warm interaction and shared moments through the day.

What’s underneath

Watching, repetition, and partial participation all deserve time.
