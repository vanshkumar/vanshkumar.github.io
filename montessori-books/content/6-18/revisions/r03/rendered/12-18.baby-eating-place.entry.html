A perspective

A place at the meal

The authors offer a low table once the baby sits steadily with feet reaching the floor. Their family-table alternative is a tray-less high chair with a baby attachment or similar.

In the everyday

You are considering how your baby takes part in mealtimes.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Around six to eight months, when sitting steadily; no upper limit for the fitted place.

Steady sitting and feet reaching the floor for the low chair; family-table alternative retained.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Baby, PDF pp. 81–82.

A low table and chair may be used for snacks, some meals or more often if that fits. The authors connect its fit with stability and participation.

They also value eating together at the family table, where the baby can be part of the conversation. Their example uses a tray-less high chair with a baby attachment or similar, qualifying the book’s earlier general preference against high chairs.
