A small invitation

A place for art to begin and rest

Keep a simple drawing invitation ready, and give wet work or saved work somewhere to go. The space can support making without keeping every piece forever.

Start by noticing

Materials are hard to find, or finished drawings keep gathering on the table.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Toddler materials are prepared together; gathering a whole set independently is a separate example around 3 years.

A simple drawing activity needs an accessible place and somewhere for finished work.

The earlier drawing example is from 12 months; independent material gathering is later. Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14, 245.

Davies describes toddler art trays with what is needed already together. Independent collecting of a whole set of materials is a later example, around 3 years.

Her options for finished work include a tray for things to return to, a selection in a scrapbook, a photograph of bulky work, or a changing display. Reusing a drawing as wrapping paper is another possibility.

Choose the arrangement that helps your family make and put things away; a separate art room is not part of the invitation.
