An everyday situation

When waiting asks too much

A waiting room or café can leave little room for a toddler’s movement and conversation. Davies suggests preparing something to share and adjusting what you expect of the visit.

In the everyday

Your child is restless while the adults need to wait.

Age, interest & context

In the book: No fixed age; the passage describes toddler movement and communication needs while waiting.

A waiting room, journey or café asks the child to wait with little room to move.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Her examples include favorite books, watching the activity outside an airport window, or a small walk together while waiting in a café.

The adult may need to be available for the child instead of reading or making a call. What helps can change during the visit; the aim is to support the child through the wait.
