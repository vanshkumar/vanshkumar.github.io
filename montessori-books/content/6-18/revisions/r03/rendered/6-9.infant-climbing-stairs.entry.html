A perspective

Exploring stairs with an adult

The authors describe supervised stair exploration for a baby who is crawling and interested in climbing. Coming down may need more time and an adult’s modeling.

In the everyday

Your baby is trying to crawl up a step or another low change in level.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Main discussion: 9 to 12 months. Stair appendix: from creeping until walking well, around 8 or 9 months.

Crawling and interest in climbing, with adult supervision and help learning descent.

The book’s ages are guides; this belongs here only while the described interest and movement fit your child.

They notice that going up can come before knowing how to get down. The monthly table describes showing backward descent.

The appendix’s stair material is a separate example: low, wide steps and a railing at either end of a bridge, listed from creeping until walking well.
