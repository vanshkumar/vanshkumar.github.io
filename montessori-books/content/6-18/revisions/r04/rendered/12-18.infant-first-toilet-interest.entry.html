A perspective

The first signs of interest in the potty

The Baby authors describe some babies becoming interested in the potty around twelve months and others much later. They follow that interest and explicitly distinguish early awareness from using the toilet independently.

In the everyday

Your baby is becoming interested in the bathroom or in what happens during a diaper change.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The room chapter says around twelve months for some babies and much later for others; the monthly table places preparatory skills at ten to twelve months. Diapering distinguishes first-year awareness from independent toileting.

Interest in the potty or bodily functions; the full passage follows the child’s actual interest.

This is an early-awareness perspective. The ten-to-twelve-month table is not a deadline or an instruction to start before interest appears.

They suggest familiar places for bathroom things and ordinary, matter-of-fact words for body parts, pee and poop. Their first-year diapering discussion presents this as learning about the body without shame.

In the room-by-room passage, offering the potty during a change follows the baby’s interest. The authors emphasize that other children will be much older when this becomes relevant.

The monthly overview mentions a potty, training pants and noticing patterns under ten to twelve months. That brief overview sits beside the fuller passage’s individual-interest condition; it does not establish an age by which a baby should use the potty.
