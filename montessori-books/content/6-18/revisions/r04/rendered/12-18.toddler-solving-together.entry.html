An everyday situation

Working out a disagreement together

Describe the problem and leave space for your child’s idea. With a young toddler, you may supply most of the possibilities while they learn how it feels to work something out together.

In the everyday

Two people want different things, or the same difficulty keeps returning.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The problem-solving passage explicitly includes preverbal toddlers.

A gesture or action may contribute; the adult may supply most ideas and retains safety responsibility.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Davies includes toddlers before fluent speech. If a crawling sibling takes a toy, her example is a toddler offering the sibling another toy to play with. A gesture or action can contribute; the adult still leads and keeps necessary limits.

Try a plan you can both live with and return to it if it is not working. A picture or brief note can also show that you heard a concern you cannot address just now.

The book’s notes are occasional aids. They do not replace the adult’s attention or the immediate action needed for safety.
