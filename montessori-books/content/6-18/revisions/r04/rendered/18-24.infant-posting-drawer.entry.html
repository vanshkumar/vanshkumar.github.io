A perspective

Post a ball, then find it

The authors describe a posting box whose ball is found by opening a drawer, using one hand to open and the other to retrieve. The ball and every part need to be suitable for mouthing.

In the everyday

Your baby is releasing objects deliberately and looking for where they have gone.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Drawer and ball: between 9 and 11+ months. Knitted-ball box: between 9 and 12+ months.

Intentional release and interest in finding an object; coordinating opening and retrieval.

This is a continuing option from the Baby book’s earlier activity list, only while it remains interesting and offers a manageable challenge.

Unlike a box with an open tray, the drawer keeps the ball out of view until the baby opens it. The baby can repeat the part that interests them.

The appendix also describes a knitted-ball version with an opening slightly smaller than its soft ball. This adds pushing the ball through before retrieving it.
