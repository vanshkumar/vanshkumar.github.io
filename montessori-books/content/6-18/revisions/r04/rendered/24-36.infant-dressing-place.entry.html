A perspective

A low place to sit while getting dressed

Once a baby sits steadily, the authors describe a very low, sturdy dressing stool with feet flat on the floor. It gives dressing a familiar place as the child takes part.

In the everyday

Your child sits steadily and is beginning to join in with dressing.

Age, interest & context

In the book: From about nine months, once sitting steadily; the passage continues into putting on pants, underwear and dressing themselves.

Steady sitting, feet flat on the floor and a sturdy very low stool that fits this child.

The low place remains useful only while its fit and the child’s dressing needs suit it.

The Baby book suggests this from about nine months, with steady sitting and the child’s actual fit guiding the choice. Its preference for a wooden stool is about sturdiness and ease of use.

The same place can support later attempts at pants, underwear and other dressing. The adult continues to offer the help needed; sitting in the dressing place does not require completing the dressing alone.

The clothing chapter pairs participation with gentle touch, a little time to respond and small choices. When it is necessary to finish, the adult can explain and help.
