A perspective

Choosing when the music plays

Junnifa describes her babies choosing to start and pause music with a reachable player button. It is a personal example of choice, alongside the authors’ preference for quiet between musical moments.

In the everyday

Your baby is interested in starting or stopping a familiar sound.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Junnifa’s example: around 8 or 9 months.

Can approach and operate the play/pause control; interested in choosing music.

The book’s ages are guides; this belongs here only while the described interest and movement fit your child.

When her babies were around 8 or 9 months, Junnifa marked the play button of a small CD player. They could approach it and later press the same button to pause.

This is one family’s example, not a needed purchase. The authors also share music through singing and listening together.
