A perspective

Follow a ball down the ramps

The authors describe a ball tracker with ramps to watch and a ball to retrieve. Their standing example requires a large, stable support, with a ball and parts suitable for mouthing.

In the everyday

Your baby watches a moving ball or wants to drop it and find it again.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Main discussion: 9 to 12 months. Appendix: from creeping, around 8 to 10+ months.

Interest in following and retrieving the ball; standing version requires independent pull-up and controlled lowering with stable support.

The book’s ages are guides; this belongs here only while the described interest and movement fit your child.

In the body example, the baby pulls up, drops the ball, bends to retrieve it, and repeats. Watching the ball is also part of the interest.

The appendix lists the activity from creeping, around 8 to 10+ months; it does not require every baby to use the standing version. The authors choose activities by the baby’s current movement and interest.
