A perspective

A turn to add and stir

When you have time to bake together, Davies includes a young toddler in adding ingredients the adult has measured and stirring with a wooden spoon. The shared process can be one small part.

In the everyday

Your child may watch you mix or reach to join the movement.

Age, interest & context

In the book: From 12 months for adding adult-measured ingredients and mixing; helping measure is a later 2½+ appendix example.

Interest in joining a small preparation movement, with the adult sharing and supervising the work.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Her baking caption gives these forms of help from 12 months. The adult has already measured the ingredients; the child can help add them or take a turn mixing.

For children under 2, she suggests keeping practical tasks to one or two steps and adding more only as they become manageable. The work will take longer, and you can stop before either of you feels overwhelmed.

The appendix’s later baking row, from 2½ years, includes helping measure ingredients. That is a different part of baking. This early invitation stays with adding and stirring alongside an adult.
