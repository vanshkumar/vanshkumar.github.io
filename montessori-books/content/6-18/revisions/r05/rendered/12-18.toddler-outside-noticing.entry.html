A small invitation

Follow a small discovery outside

Pause for the moving leaves, a cloud, or an insect at work. A walk can make room for what your child notices.

Start by noticing

They may stop, point, listen, or return to a tiny detail.

Age, interest & context

In the book: No fixed age in the outdoor-noticing passage; it addresses toddlers.

Interest in a detail, sound, movement or quiet moment outdoors.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Davies invites noticing colors, wind, droplets on leaves, and the sounds and smells outdoors. There can also be a quiet moment of looking together.

When you have time, follow your child’s pace. A discovery need not become a lesson: you can wonder together or find a name later. The book includes nearby parks and other places beyond home for families without outdoor space.
