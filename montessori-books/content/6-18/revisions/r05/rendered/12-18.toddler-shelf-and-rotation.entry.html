A small invitation

Make activities easy to see and choose

Set out a few complete activities your child is interested in, with their pieces together and a familiar place to return them.

Start by noticing

Notice what gets used, what is too difficult, and what has stopped inviting another turn.

Age, interest & context

In the book: No fixed age; the source explicitly describes adult modeling and shared return for young toddlers.

The child can see and choose familiar activities; the adult notices interest or difficulty.

Putting things away remains shared; the child need not complete a whole work cycle. Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14, 33.

A basket or tray can show what belongs together. Davies sets out a puzzle undone and keeps needed pieces available; an incomplete activity can be put aside until its missing piece is found.

Store unused or currently difficult activities for another time, and rotate when your child is looking for a new challenge. The book does not ask for a fixed number or a rotation schedule.

Show where things belong and share putting them back. If your child is already absorbed in the next activity, Davies sometimes models the return herself.
