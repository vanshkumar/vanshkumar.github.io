A perspective

Beginning solid foods

The authors describe first foods as gradual shared exploration, waiting until six months and signs of readiness. Their account pairs manageable food and the baby’s pace with choking precautions and learning first aid.

In the everyday

Your baby is showing interest in food and meals.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Six months and signs of readiness; the book describes food exploration from six months to one year.

Interest in food and meals while early solid-food experiences are unfolding.

They describe noticing a food’s smell and texture, hearing its name and seeing it prepared. Eating can bring language and purposeful hand use together.

Their preference is for food the baby can manage with help suited to their coordination, rather than being hurried to an adult’s pace. The book’s practical food examples sit beside choking precautions and a recommendation to learn first aid.

The authors’ earlier descriptions of food interest are not the same as their explicit six-month-plus-readiness condition for starting solids.
