A small invitation

Stay with a favorite page

Share a simple book about familiar people or things. Follow the page that interests your baby, even if you never finish the book.

Start by noticing

Your baby looks closely, reaches for a page, or responds to a familiar picture.

Age, interest & context

In the book: First-year book guidance; appendix Books row says All ages.

Interest in pictures, pages or familiar subjects; follow the baby’s chosen page.

The activity may continue while it fits your child’s interest and current skills; the source ages are guides.

The authors suggest sturdy board books, simple pictures and subjects from daily life. Describe what your baby is looking at, or enjoy the picture together.

A small book of family photographs is another possibility. Let page turning and looking be part of the experience; reading backward or returning to one page can belong too.
