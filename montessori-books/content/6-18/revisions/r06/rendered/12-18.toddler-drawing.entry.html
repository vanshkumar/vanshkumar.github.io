A small invitation

Make a mark

Offer paper and a chunky pencil or crayon that makes an easy mark. Your child can explore lines and movement without making a picture of anything.

Start by noticing

Notice an interest in making marks and whether the drawing material is comfortable to hold.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Scribbling: from 12 months.

Interest in making marks with a drawing tool the child can hold.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Toddler, PDF pp. 14.

Protect the table and leave the marks to your child. If you draw together, use your own paper. When showing the material, a loose line or squiggle leaves plenty for them to discover.

When your child looks to you, you can describe a line or color you notice, or ask if they would like to tell you about it. The drawing need not stand for a recognizable object.

Try it together

  1. Put out paper and a chunky pencil or crayon that glides easily.
  2. If needed, show a loose line on your own paper.
  3. Let your child explore, with more paper available if they want another turn.
