A small invitation

Share a little of the cleanup

At a meal already underway, offer a cloth and let your baby join a small part of wiping. You can finish what still needs doing.

Start by noticing

Your baby is interested in the cloth or in your wiping movement.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The book gives an eight-month table-wiping example with imperfect results.

Interest in a cloth or the adult’s wiping during an existing meal.

The authors describe a cloth for hands and mouth and a little shared table wiping. Their eight-month-old example is imperfect, with the adult still doing cleanup.

This is participation in an ordinary moment, not a task your baby needs to finish.
