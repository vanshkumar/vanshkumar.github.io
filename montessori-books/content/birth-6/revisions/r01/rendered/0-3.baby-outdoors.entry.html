A small invitation

Pause to watch and listen outside

During time outdoors, pause over the leaves, birds or passing life your baby notices. A few names and some quiet can be enough.

Start by noticing

Something outside holds their gaze or makes them turn to listen.

Age, interest & context

In the book: From as early as the first month.

A sight or sound outdoors holds the baby’s attention.

The invitation is to look and listen together; it does not require grasping natural objects or moving independently.

The authors describe the movement of leaves and the sounds of birds as things a baby can enjoy. We can name a tree, dog or vehicle, then leave room to look and listen together.
