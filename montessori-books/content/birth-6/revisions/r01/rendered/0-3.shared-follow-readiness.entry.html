A perspective

Choose activities that fit your child

The books use activity ages as guides. They suggest choosing by your child’s present interest and effort, while keeping support available if you have concerns about development.

In the everyday

When an age label makes you wonder whether an activity is a fit.

Age, interest & context

Choose by current interest and effort.

Activity ages are guides; this is help with choosing, not a skill to reach.

Look at what your child is trying, what holds their attention, and how they respond to a challenge. The books suggest setting aside an activity that is too easy or discouragingly difficult and returning to it when it is a better fit.

Their purpose is to support a child’s own development. The Baby authors explicitly say to seek support for developmental concerns and explain that specialist guidance is beyond their book. Davies likewise includes learning about differences and getting needed support.

What’s underneath

Use ages to find possibilities and observation to consider the fit.
