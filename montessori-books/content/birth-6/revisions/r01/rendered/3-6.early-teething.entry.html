A perspective

When teeth begin to appear

The authors describe teething as easy for some babies and uncomfortable for others. Their account includes comfort and care for a newly emerged tooth, while their wider discussion keeps medical advice available for crying or pain.

In the everyday

You are noticing drool or caring for a tooth that has appeared.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The book gives no starting age for teething.

For a tooth that has appeared, or a need for comfort; no tooth is expected at a particular age.

Keeping a cloth nearby to wipe the chin is one ordinary comfort they mention. Their examples of changes around teething are not a reason to assign every difficult spell to a tooth.

For newly emerged teeth, they describe cleaning with a soft cloth or toothbrush and water.

When crying is prolonged or a baby seems to be in pain, their broader care section describes sharing observations with a doctor or pediatrician and seeking medical advice.
