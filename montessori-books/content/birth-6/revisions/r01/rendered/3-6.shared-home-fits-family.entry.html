A perspective

Let the home fit your family

Start with the child and the home you have. The books describe useful surroundings that change with family life, without requiring a collection of special furniture or toys.

In the everyday

When setting up the home starts to feel like another large project.

Age, interest & context

When considering a change to your home.

General guidance for the home you share with your baby.

Look at what is helping and what is making everyday life harder. A useful change might make something easier to find, reduce repeated tidying, or give an interested child more room to take part.

The Baby authors say Montessori can be practiced without buying special materials. Davies describes homes that retain their character, cultures, and traditions, including when space is shared or small.

People belong to this environment too: their presence, language, and relationships matter alongside the physical space.

What’s underneath

The surroundings serve the child and the people who live there.
