More than one language is part of the baby’s family or community.

The book offers different arrangements for different families.

One example gives each caregiver a language to use with the baby. Another connects languages with particular places or times, such as home, outings or weekends.

Books, play and conversation with people who speak a language are further possibilities. These arrangements need to fit the people and resources a family has.
