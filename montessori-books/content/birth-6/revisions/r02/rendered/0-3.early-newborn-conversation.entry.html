A small invitation

Begin with your voice

Use your baby’s name and tell them what is happening. Pause so a sound or gesture can become part of the conversation.

Start by noticing

You are holding or caring for your baby, with a moment to talk and listen.

Age, interest & context

In the book: 0 to 3 months; from shortly after birth.

An ordinary moment of holding or care, with time for a sound or gesture.

Early exchanges can include quiet pauses; no particular response is required.

A bath about to begin, the part of the body being washed, or the room you are entering can give you something to talk about. Use the words you know and speak gently.

Let your baby see your face. After saying something, wait and notice their response. You might echo a sound or answer a gesture, then leave another pause. The book treats these early exchanges as conversation.

If your baby has an older sibling, another conversation can begin while that child plays: with the baby awake, talk about what their sibling is doing.
