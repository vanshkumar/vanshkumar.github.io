A perspective

Clothes that let your baby move

The authors favor soft, comfortable clothes that let a baby move easily. They look for an easy opening for dressing and a fit that is neither tight nor so loose that it creases or tangles.

In the everyday

You are choosing everyday clothes or noticing how an outfit feels as your baby moves.

Age, interest & context

Choose with your baby’s current movements and comfort in mind.

Wrap tops or a wider shoulder opening are among their examples. Their concern is how the clothes feel against the baby’s skin and how easily the adult can dress them.

They prefer simple clothing with few hard fastenings or decorations. Separate tops and trousers, with socks if needed, are examples they give for freer movement.

These are the authors’ clothing preferences, with comfort and movement at the center. Dressing itself can remain a gentle exchange with the baby.
