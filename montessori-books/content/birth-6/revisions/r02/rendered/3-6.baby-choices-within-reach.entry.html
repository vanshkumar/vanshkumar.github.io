A home within reach

← Three starting ideas

A small invitation

Make the choices easy to see

Keep a few current favorites visible and reachable. Watch what your baby returns to, and change the selection when interest or challenge changes.

Start by noticing

Your baby is choosing between objects or moving toward something they want.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The 3 to 6 months movement section; “5 or 6+ months” for the familiar-toy basket.

The baby is interested in visible objects and beginning to choose or move toward one.

A few current objects can be enough; the adult restores them after play.

The book suggests a low shelf or basket with a small selection, each object easy to notice. Materials that are too difficult, too easy or no longer interesting can rest out of sight for another time.

At this stage, the adult restores the materials after play. A baby does not need to put one thing away before exploring another.

In a shared room, the authors keep older children’s small parts where the baby cannot reach or open them, while leaving baby-suitable objects accessible.
