A small invitation

A conversation with your baby

Answer a sound, smile or gesture, then pause. Your baby’s next response can guide the conversation.

Start by noticing

Your baby is watching your face or experimenting with sounds.

Age, interest & context

In the book: 3 to 6 months; the sign discussion follows 5 to 6 months, while the summary lists signs at 7 to 9 months.

Interest in a face, mouth movement or vocal exchange.

The book’s sign ages differ; its main invitation is to answer the baby’s present communication.

Come where your baby can see your face. Say something, listen, and leave a little space for their sound or movement. You can echo a sound, smile back, or put a few words to what seems to interest them.

Raspberries, bubbles and changes in volume are part of the vocal play the authors describe. There is no need to ask for a particular sound or word.

For families who choose baby signs, the book suggests pairing a few familiar spoken words with signs, such as “milk” or “done.” Its age labels differ between sections; the authors’ wider guidance is to notice the baby’s response and interest.
