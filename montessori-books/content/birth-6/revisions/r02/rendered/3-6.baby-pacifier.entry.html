A perspective

The book’s view of pacifiers

The authors discourage constant pacifier use while also acknowledging a possible reduction in SIDS risk and allowing limited use for settling or sleep. Their position is not an absolute ban.

In the everyday

You are reading about pacifiers in the Montessori approach.

Their concern is that something constantly in the mouth can limit opportunities for sounds and communication. The same discussion recognizes that a pacifier may help an unsettled baby or a baby falling asleep.

The book also includes considering a pacifier at naps and bedtime in its 2021 sleep-safety box. These parts belong together when understanding the authors’ view.
