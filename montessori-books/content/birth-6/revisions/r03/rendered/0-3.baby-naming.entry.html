A small invitation

Name what caught their attention

When your baby turns toward a sound or studies something nearby, give it a name and leave room for them to listen.

Start by noticing

Their gaze settles on an object, or they turn when a familiar sound begins.

Age, interest & context

In the book: Naming surroundings appears at 0 to 3 months; the detailed sound-and-object examples appear at 6 to 9 months.

The baby’s attention settles on something you can name.

Use the names you know. This is shared noticing, without asking the baby to repeat a word. Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Baby, PDF pp. 119–120, 130.

A barking dog, a ringing phone or a spoon in your hand can start the conversation. Use the name you know and a few ordinary words about what is happening.

Follow the interest already there. The authors’ examples are shared noticing, not a request for the baby to repeat the word.
