A small invitation

Read a little of your book aloud

In the first few months, a book you want to read can become something to share: read it aloud with your baby nearby.

Start by noticing

You have something you would enjoy reading and your baby is awake and comfortable.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The first few months, within the 0 to 3 months discussion.

Your baby is awake and comfortable while you read aloud nearby.

This is an early way to share your reading; simple picture books can join it as interest changes.

Davies and Uzodike describe early reading as a chance to hear the sounds and rhythm of language. The book does not have to be a baby book; you can read from one that interests you.

Simple picture books are another option. If a picture catches your baby’s attention, you can describe it or stay with that page.
