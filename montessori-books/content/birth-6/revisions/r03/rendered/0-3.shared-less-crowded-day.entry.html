Age, interest & context

When daily work and time with your baby compete.

The book includes both the early weeks and everyday life beyond them. Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Baby, PDF pp. 224.

Look at what matters to your family and what could be simplified or postponed. In The Montessori Baby, doing your best changes with the day, including how much rest you have had.

Davies acknowledges work, money, and circumstances that cannot simply be changed. She describes finding opportunities during everyday routines or on a weekend, rather than requiring the same day from every family.

A meal, a conversation, or some time together can be part of family life you are already living. The books’ examples offer possibilities, not a schedule to complete.
