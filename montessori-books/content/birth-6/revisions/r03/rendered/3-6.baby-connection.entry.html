A small invitation

Let them know before a pickup

When a pickup can wait, come into view, say what you have in mind, and pause for a response. Necessary care or protection still calls for gentle action.

Start by noticing

You are about to pick your baby up, and there is time to make it an exchange.

Age, interest & context

When the pickup can wait, leave time for whatever response your baby gives.

Age and readiness sources: The Montessori Baby, PDF pp. 285–289.

Come where your baby can see you and let them know you would like to pick them up. Wait and notice their expression or movement. If they turn away and the pickup can wait, give them more time.

Their response is something to listen to, not a certain yes-or-no signal. When care, comfort or protection is needed, the authors describe telling the baby what is happening and acting gently.
