A perspective

Bath time as a moment together

The authors describe bathing as gentle, supported care shared with the baby, with talking and eye contact. For a newborn, they explicitly keep the head supported throughout.

In the everyday

You are helping your baby through a bath.

Age, interest & context

For a baby who is being bathed with adult care and support.

They describe familiar, economical movements and having supplies ready, including an open towel to receive the baby. A repeated sequence can help the adult handle the baby with confidence.

Talking, smiling and looking at the baby belong to the care in their account. Their emphasis is on gentle touch and adequate support, especially while a newborn is being handled.
