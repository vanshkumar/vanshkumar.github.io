A small invitation

Watch a hand’s shadow

Hold your hand with light behind it and let its shadow offer something for your baby to notice.

Start by noticing

Your baby is awake, comfortable and interested in looking.

Age, interest & context

In the book: The visual mobiles discussion is placed at 0 to 3 months; the months 3 to 4 summary continues visual watching.

Awake, comfortable interest in watching light, shadow or movement.

Follow the interest you see, without a set looking time.

The authors include hand shadows among the simple things a young baby can watch. Notice where their gaze settles and give them time to look.

They also describe light and shadow moving through leaves and branches, or a high-contrast picture within the baby’s view. Follow the interest you see; looking away or becoming upset is a reason to stop or change what is offered.
