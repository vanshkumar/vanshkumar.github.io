## Decisions and qualifications for review

- Observation, activity selection, partial participation and time remain
  distinct. Observation stays factual/tentative; help and protection remain
  available. No performance test, processing timer or completion requirement
  was created.
- The participation entry selects the general relational meaning from the held
  Baby collaboration evidence. It publishes no fork/feeding, newborn latch,
  rolling or positioning example. The original research hold remains recorded;
  exact reviewers must judge this bounded selection instead of treating the
  research status as a pass or an automatic exclusion.
- Adult needs have substantive space: practical help, a visible help-list
  example attributed to Baby, access limits, rest, wider support and professional
  help for depression. Doctor/help wording is visible in both summary and detail.
  No named-provider equivalence or therapeutic music-frequency claim is used.
- Adult interests/personal practices are separate from depression support.
  Their optional, nonuniversal character is visible. The books' calm lists do
  not become a regimen, a requirement to get up early, or a parent score.
- The adult pause includes the immediate-danger exception in its short summary
  and retains responsive care in detail. No stepping-away setup, mandated
  countdown, child isolation or calm prerequisite is authored.
- Adult repair owns the adult's action and offers apology/another response;
  it does not ask the child to soothe the adult or promise immediate forgiveness.
- Ordinary caregiver discussion has a mutually wanted/trusted-caregiver cue.
  The source's two different communication exercises are not merged into a
  timed procedure or an obligation to reconcile. Family-separation copy visibly
  retains the explicit safety exception to contact.
- Family time respects actual work, money and support. Source schedules,
  imagined perfect arrangements and deterministic outcomes are not reader
  requirements. The Baby help-list and Toddler rushed-day/update examples are
  identified as their respective book's examples where they differ.
- No image is proposed. General principles do not authorize a physical setup.
  Any later scene or key-moment illustration needs its own grounded review.
