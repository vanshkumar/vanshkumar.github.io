# The Montessori Baby research package

Source: `baby-2021`, the supplied 310-page PDF described in
[`SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`](../foundation/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md). All references use
one-based physical PDF pages; there is no printed-page offset.

This directory contains task 2 research for the approved birth-through-age-three
companion. It contains source evidence and editorial dispositions, not polished
app copy or publication approval. The final status and exact coverage are in
`HANDOFF.md`; independent completeness findings are in `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md`.

## File guide

- `SOURCE_MAP.md`: source-order orientation, original-PDF identity, visual traps
  and navigation into the detailed registers.
- `PAGE_REGISTER.json`: one record per physical page, with section and read-log
  links. It does not treat a cached extraction as a human read.
- `SECTION_REGISTER.json`: source-order substantive sections, callouts, tables,
  appendices and non-advice material, with reading and visual obligations.
- `READ_LOG.json` and `READ_LOG.md`: exact reader identities, spans and modes.
- `COVERAGE_LEDGER.jsonl`: each source idea's included, combined, context-only or
  excluded disposition, its reason, evidence and surviving targets.
- `EVIDENCE.jsonl`: qualified claims, exact references, source age/readiness,
  proposed topics/kinds, sensitivities, cross-references and held questions.
- `CROSSWALK.json`: resolved within-book links and repeated-source witnesses;
  source differences remain visible.
- `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`: material holds, source tensions and downstream decisions.
- `VALIDATION.json`: mechanical coverage/reference checks and final data hashes.
- `sections/`: named readers' source-section inputs and detailed notes. The root
  consolidated records are the handoff view; partition files retain provenance.

Included means retained as a research candidate, not authored or approved.
Combined preserves a distinct detail in a named surviving idea. Context-only
records preserve theory, examples, summaries or caveats without requiring separate
reader entries. Excluded records retain exact source locators and reasons.

Research-ready is not a publication pass. Held records name the unresolved
interpretation or missing specification. No outside medical or safety guidance
was imported to repair a book claim. Source summaries, apparatus and photographs
must not be converted into an operational protocol by shortening them.

Task 4 uses this package alongside the complete Toddler handoff to define content
and reviewed pilots. Every final entry, including the six prototypes, still needs
fresh independent xhigh source and spirit/tone reviews of its exact revision and
age/context presentation.
