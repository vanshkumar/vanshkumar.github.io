# Tone calibration

These are original editorial paraphrases for calibration, not quotations,
publication-ready entries, or substitutes for reading the cited source. Their
scope is deliberately small. None has the two independent content reviews needed
for publication. The full source-note files retain further qualifications and
family contexts; later authors must use complete evidence records.

## Editing checks tied to these examples

- **Do not add a result.** "This builds lifelong confidence" is a new claim unless
  the exact source and qualification support it, and deterministic source claims
  still need explicit disposition rather than promotion as guarantees.
- **Do not strengthen the verb.** A possibility does not become an instruction
  that every family must follow. A source age does not become "by this age."
- **Do not remove the concrete help.** "Trust yourself" alone would lose the
  observation, practical adjustment, available assistance or repair that makes
  these examples useful.
- **Do not cool the relationship.** Leaving room for concentration does not mean
  refusing warmth, withholding help or prohibiting all acknowledgment.
- **Do not add a burden to the adult.** Reflection, participation and preparation
  are possibilities, not a new performance checklist or a promise of a compliant
  child.
- **Do not let attribution hide a missing condition.** A sensitive claim preceded
  by an author's name still needs its relevant context and cautions.

The two source-note files contain additional examples, source tensions and exact
read logs. They are available for research calibration under the same unpublished
status, not an extra bank of preapproved copy.
