## Table and visual completeness

The activity ledger accounts for all 107 appendix rows and 66 practical-life chart
bullets, including 16 bullets in the 3-to 4 column and 21 appendix 3+ rows. Those later
variants remain excluded rather than being shifted into 2-3 browsing. Broad rows
with several distinct examples have subordinate accounting. Every numbered
activity photo is paired with its full facing-page caption; images do not supply
an inferred child age or permission for unsupervised use.

Core accounts for every sensitive-period row, all eleven observation-checklist
categories, all nine room labels, every illustrated starter item and all six
Austria-tour pages. Care/limits accounts for each substantive care unit, complete
recaps/checklists, concentration-list continuation, all daily-care/change sections
and the coat-flip visual. Adult/family accounts for all adult/family subsections,
all 24 units of the compressed language table, the complete feelings/needs table,
both recipe variants and method/storage qualifications, all six tours and all
reference/backmatter pages. Fifty bibliography/material-source entries are
separately inventoried without importing external sources.

Sparse or empty extraction was resolved against original pages. In particular,
32,74,94,116,144,186,202 and 231 are illustrations;87 is a photograph. These are
not blank-page shortcuts. Photos 59/61/63/65/67 depend on captions 60/62/64/66/68.
The read log distinguishes structural visual inventory from readable individual
page/detail checks.

## Source-order accounting and interpretation

Every substantive idea has an included, combined, context-only or excluded
ledger disposition with a reason and concrete target. Included/combined records
trace evidence. The 106 read logs preserve who actually read which exact span and
mode; the root assembler takes no credit for reading peer spans. The coverage
counts describe completed research, not a target number of app entries.

`CROSS_REFERENCES.jsonl` retains recorded source/context links and explicit
cross-part destinations. External resources marked inventoried/not-read are
reference accounting only. No essential internal destination remains unread;
interpretive questions and age/safety holds remain explicit in evidence and
`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
