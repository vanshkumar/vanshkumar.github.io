The scope is larger than materials. Practical life retains slower work, imperfect

uses separate adult/child papers; conversation includes presence, quiet and time
to communicate. Home play can include selected existing open-ended toys, and
school materials need not be recreated at home. The chapter's closing is an
invitation to start modestly, not a compulsory activity program.
