An exhortation.

Breaking down each word:
- Always – no exceptions
- Do – let it occur without hesitation
- What feels right – feel whatever is happening across all of the senses

Notably, it is not to *only* do what feels right to *you*. It is to do what feels right to the *system*, to Nature. But don't forget that you are also a part of Nature.

Trust in your ability to get better at noticing when something feels right over time.

 Equivalently, [return everything to nature](https://luminousdharma.org/returning-to-nature-advanced-dharma-practice/):
 - Wish sincerely to nature, "Please handle this"
 - Stop fiddling with things and wait, noticing whatever is happening across all of the senses right now
 - Experience it all and keep a core motivation or wish to sense what feels like the best fit
	 - When something that feels like a wise fit arises, let it occur without hesitation. Observe how these phenomena and actions play out
	 - When something that arises does not feel wise to enact, let it pass untouched, and it will unwind on its own
 - When we find we are entangled or caught again, restart the process and wish again, "Please handle this"

The same core idea from different angles:
- Be impulsive, it's the only way to generate better impulses over time
- [You want what you actually do](https://newsletter.pathlesspath.com/p/you-want-what-you-actually-do-296), so you are *already* acting in accordance with your wants. Lean into it.
- Try seeing yourself as the natural intelligence you are, without self-awareness (a la [[Blindsight]]). Give the manager self a break, it deserves it.
