An exhortation.

Breaking down each word:
- Always – no exceptions
- Do – let it occur without hesitation
- What feels right – feel whatever is happening across all of the senses

Notably, it is not to *only* do what feels right to *you*. It is to do what feels right to the *system*, to Nature. But don't forget that you are also a part of Nature. Trust in your ability to get better over time at noticing when something feels right.

How do you actually do this?
1. [[Return it to Nature]]
2. If it cannot be returned to Nature, do good deeds
