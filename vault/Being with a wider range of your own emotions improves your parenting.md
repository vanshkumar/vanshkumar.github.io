The Art of Accomplishment folks suggest that it's difficult to handle emotions in others that you can't handle in yourself. I find this generally true from my own experience.

I want my kids to relate well to their emotions, welcoming and accepting them all. While I can't control the majority of what happens in their life & therefore how they end up relating to their emotions, I want to do my part of being able to be with all their emotions as they come up. This means practicing being with the emotions in myself that I struggle with, such as:
- Anger
- Fear
- Helplessness
- Grief
- Joy

This is part of why I took [[The AoA Connection Course]], and why I am noting things like [[Self-reliance is likely holding me back]].
