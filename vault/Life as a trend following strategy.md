A silly analogy that popped into my head.

The default view of life seems to be something closer to a stock/bond style allocation – a large buy-and-hold style bet on stocks/bonds doing well. A trend following view would be like constantly placing well-sized bets that cancel out on average, but once in a blue moon do very well. The distinction is a frozen choice vs frequent ongoing bets, passive vs active.

The return curve of a trend following strategy is notoriously difficult to deal with psychologically, because most of the time it's flat or losing slightly, but once in a while it pops off. We don't seem to be wired for handling that kind of return curve well, which is perhaps why there is still juice in trend following in the first place.
