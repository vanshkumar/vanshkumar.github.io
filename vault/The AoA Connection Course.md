I have attempted to take the [AoA Connection Course](https://www.artofaccomplishment.com/course/the-connection-course) twice before. The first time in 2024, I forget exactly what happened, there was a change in my schedule or something, so I deferred my enrollment. The second time in 2025, I did the first session and then had some anxiety/a feeling of oddness in my system so I decided to drop out and defer it again. Finally on my third go around I am completing the course. I'm glad I am!

I'm not quite sure what prompted me to follow through with the course now. Part of it was a feeling that it will get harder and harder to find time to do it as we have more kids. Part of it was that I believe that [[Being with a wider range of your own emotions improves your parenting]], and I want to be a better parent. Another big part was a series of 3 coaching sessions I had with [Drew Smith](https://www.unfoldi.ng/coaching) (who was offering free sessions through the [Pathless Community](https://community.pathlesspath.com/)), which I was apprehensive about but ended up enjoying a lot. I have listened to a bunch of the AoA podcasts and tried some of their techniques on my own, but the coaching sessions were the first time I put them into practice with another person. It was *so* different. Much more vulnerable and enjoyable.

The AoA course has been more of that. I recall seeing someone describe it as an "emotional gym," which is extremely apt. It involves both feeling emotions and experimenting with relating to them in different ways in a safe space. Things like sharing vulnerable aspects of your life with a stranger and feeling into your body while doing it, intentionally triggering each other to practice responding differently, stating your agenda in conversations, externalizing the voice in your head. It's been emotional, tear-inducing, tiring, raw, and a lot of fun. I am motivated to keep practicing & become more emotionally fluid over time, especially with loved ones rather than just with strangers.

Taking the majority of the course while we were visiting my family in Nova made it exceptionally clear how much emotional work there is for me to do. During charged conversations with my mom and others, it was very difficult to keep the points from the course in mind. I bottled multiple opportunities to stay in VIEW and have deeper connection.

At the same time, taking this course did help me notice how much progress I have made in the last two years since quitting my job. The voice in my head is significantly quieter & occasionally even encouraging now, I am more often aware of my emotional state, and I am better about looking at problems in my life from many different angles. I can see a clear arc of emotional progress looking back, starting from processing quitting at the same time as finding out Kathy was pregnant ([[10.1.2024|more details]]). My internal experience has definitely improved.

---

One of the biggest takeaways from the course has been how important it is to do this work with other people. My default approach ([[Self-reliance could be holding me back]]) is to try to figure things out myself, which has been useful for learning the AoA content over the last couple years, but the practice has to be with others. It's annoying to think about how much more progress I could have made if I had done this course or coaching sooner.

More tactical techniques from the course:
- Ask how/what questions, they are much more open-ended
	- Feel the wonder in your body in conversations & ask from that place ([[Wonder as guiding principle]])
- State your agenda ("I'm trying to fix you right now and I'm sorry") and own your wants ("I want you to like me")
	- This is extremely scary to even think about doing with loved ones!! To start, just ask "what's my agenda?" and notice it
- Keep some awareness in your body (I like soles of my feet) in conversations, it keeps you grounded and able to be with your own emotions while still being with others
- When triggered, settle your nervous system & ask how/what questions from a place of deep self-acceptance
- What's your vulnerable scary truth right now? Say it
