A while ago I built a [simple pairwise ranking app](https://vanshkumar.net/terminal-desires-ranker/) for figuring out "terminal desires" – desires that are ends in themselves, things you do for their own sake. My top terminal desire by far was curiosity, which [[Terminal|made a lot of sense to me at the time]].

But thinking about it further, the feeling I enjoy is actually closer to _astonishment_ or _wonder._ The feeling in your body of complete and utter awe.

While doing the AoA Connection course, they describe the difference between wonder and curiosity as awe & not needing an answer. Living in the question rather than searching specifically for an answer. The feeling of awe is familiar to me, but not looking for an answer feels strange.

I wonder what it would be like to fully accept wonder as a navigational principle in my life?
