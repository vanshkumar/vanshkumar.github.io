what a tangled web we weave
the silk of our thoughts feelings hopes and dreams
a perfect structure that we can’t bear to leave,
until it’s complete and time has slipped through its seams

eight legs working overtime, never satisfied
a focus so complete it shuts out reality
waiting until the moment is right, sharp-eyed
not noticing our impending mortality
