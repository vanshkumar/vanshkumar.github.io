in a world not yet awake
why do I claim to be?
am I pretentious for even conceiving of myself
as separate from the world
unhappiness is wishing things were otherwise
loneliness is ignorance of one's place in the
world, a delusion of separateness
there exists no isolated system
only imaginary boundaries we place to make
things easier to understand,
not understanding that nothing can be understood in
isolation
rise with the sun
lend an ear to the squabbling parrots
look at nothing in particular
and see it all
