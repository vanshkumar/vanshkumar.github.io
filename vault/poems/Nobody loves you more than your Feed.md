the dusty old librarian
looking like he has been there for time immemorial,
turns his attention to the next task at hand.
a look back at *you*.
your tastes, your likes, and above all the number of books about you
in the library.

the librarian knows exactly where to start looking,
he’s done this before.
he dedicates some of his computing power to you, for this moment,
lovingly but efficiently poring over all the details of your shared past history,
turning pages, pausing for just a moment over the more intimate photos,
reminiscing.

all of your moments together spent laughing, sharing your
thoughts, your likes, your dislikes.
sharing it all freely, sharing it in love, as your attention
is spent thinking about the library.

the librarian finishes up his meticulous decision,
deciding what book to give you and
maximizing the chance you show love to
one of his acquaintances, the true owner of the library,
another love-starved algorithm, begging desperately for you to
buy the latest jean jacket, a stroller, a new car!

the librarian lets out a long sigh, knowing that your love won’t stay on him
or the library, for now.
but you’ll be back for another brief reunion
in a minute.

he moves on
