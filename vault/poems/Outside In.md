Does death come from the outside in?
Knocking at your soul, throwing stones at your core
Ripples cementing themselves as wrinkles over time
Or is it from the inside out?
An invisible degradation at the cellular level
Which is only eventually seen on the surface

Slowly becoming another being
Are you unrecognizable yet unchanged
Or transfigured but still discernible

If you do turn yellow from the edges
And eventually brown and blow away in the wind
Recall that it doesn’t matter, every second and every minute and every year
You were always changing anyway
